What We Know About Vignesh and Triken

Vignesh does not want random meetings. He wants qualified buyers who already understand why Triken may be relevant and who have a real product explanation problem.

Triken's strongest public proof is not generic animation. It is clarity for complex products and environments where accuracy matters.

Healthcare Medtech Enterprise technology Scientific animation Engineering visualisation Event and launch visuals

Credibility to use carefully: 10 plus years, 400 plus projects from the query form, and public work or brand proof from Triken's site including Accenture, EY, DBS, Samsung, Deloitte, Siemens Healthineers, SingHealth, HP, A*Star, and enterprise or healthcare case examples.

The Two Packages

Package 1: Product Story Mapping Session

When to use: The prospect has a complex product, campaign, or launch, but no clear sign yet that a full video budget is active.

Message job: Sell the cost of unclear thinking before production.

Best buyers: Product marketing, head of product, marketing director, communications lead.

CTA: Ask if mapping the story before production would be useful.

Package 2: Full Animation Production

When to use: The prospect has an obvious launch, campaign, event, product education need, or existing use of video.

Message job: Sell Triken as the team that can turn complex product detail into clear, accurate visuals.

Best buyers: Marketing, communications, product marketing, commercial leader.

CTA: Ask if they already have a product video or launch asset planned.

Most teams do not lose time because animation is hard.

Product explains it one way.
Marketing explains it another way.
Sales has to simplify it again on calls.

That is exactly what our Product Story Mapping Session is built for.

In 90 minutes, we turn decks, product notes, and existing material into a clear Product Blueprint before anyone starts animating.

Would that be useful before {{company}} builds the next product video or campaign asset?

M3: Package 2 Angle

If there is already a video or launch asset planned, Triken can also handle the full production.

Concept.
Script.
Storyboard.
Animation.
Revision management.

That is normally a 10k to 18k project for standard B2B explainers.

But I would not suggest starting there unless the story is already clear.

For {{company}}, is the bigger issue the story map first, or the actual production?

M4: Close Loop

I will close the loop after this, {{first_name}}.

If {{company}} has no product video, launch, or buyer education push coming up, timing may be wrong.

If one is coming, the useful first step is usually to map the story before production starts.

Should I leave this for now, or is it worth a short look?

Qualification Before Passing To Vignesh

That sounds relevant.

Before I suggest a time, can I check three things?

What product or campaign are you trying to explain better?

Is the current need story clarity, full animation production, or both?

And is there a timeline attached to it?

Operational Rule Going Forward
