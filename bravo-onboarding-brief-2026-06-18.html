Pain map

The founder is the ceiling

  • Hiring with no real process.
  • Bad fits causing early churn.
  • No performance or feedback framework.
  • No leadership layer beneath the founder.
  • Every people problem routes back to the founder.
  • The company stalls around predictable thresholds such as 30, 50, and 150 people.
Solution map

Playbooks plus judgment plus momentum

  • Expert built HR and leadership playbooks.
  • One to one coaching.
  • Group coaching.
  • Weekly check ins.
  • Lower cost than a full HR hire or classic consultancy.

Campaign thesis

Do not lead with “HR consulting.” Lead with “your people process is still in your head.”

At 15 people, the founder can still hold the people process together. Around 30 to 50 people, that starts breaking. TalentBSA installs the hiring, feedback, performance, and leadership rhythms before churn and management debt get expensive.

Lead sourcing signals

What a good prospect should show

  • Hiring multiple roles.
  • Founder posting about hiring, culture, delegation, leadership, team growth, or churn.
  • Funding, expansion, new market, or new office signal.
  • Team moved past 15, 30, or 50 employees.
  • No visible senior HR leader.
  • Founder still visibly involved in people decisions.
