B · M3regulatory audit trail, the strongest B message

Hey {fn},

One thing becoming a real headache for credit teams. The EU AI Act now treats credit scoring as high risk, so banks have to explain the data going into a model, not just the result.

Most data cleaning leaves no record of what was changed. deeploans logs every step by default, so when an auditor asks, you can show your work.

Is that on your radar yet?

Farhan
