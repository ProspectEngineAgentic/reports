Positioning

Triken should not be positioned as a cheaper animation vendor. The campaign should sell the upstream strategy problem.

Core angle: Before a team spends five figures on a product video, they need a product story clear enough to animate.

Offers

Product Story Mapping Session

  • 90-minute live working session.
  • Uses Triken's AI-powered Blueprint Canvas.
  • Turns decks, PDFs, product notes, and website copy into a Product Blueprint.
  • Same-day deliverable.
  • Credited against animation production if they proceed within 30 days.

Full Animation Production

  • Concept, scripting, storyboarding, animation, and revision management.

Lead List Building Steps

  1. Build the first 100 reviewed leads before launch.
  2. Start with Singapore medtech, biotech, healthcare technology, pharma, AI SaaS, fintech, and industrial technology.
  3. Require a clear trigger such as product launch, funding, new campaign, new product page, event, hiring in product marketing, or market expansion.
  4. Collect 2 to 3 contacts per target company when possible.
  5. Enrich emails with Apollo.
  6. Mark LinkedIn degree. First-degree goes to warm outreach. Second and third-degree go to connection plus email lanes.
  7. Reject vendors, animation studios, agencies, freelancers, job seekers, investors, and collaboration-only profiles.

LinkedIn Flow

  1. Silent connection request unless a note is manually approved.
  2. M1 after accept: short opener around complex product explanation.
  3. M2 after 4 days: explain that many video problems are really story problems.
  4. M3 after 7 days: introduce the 90-minute Product Story Mapping Session.
  5. M4 after 10 days: soft close.
  6. Stop on any reply and qualify before booking.

Email Flow

  1. Email 1: product story problem.
  2. Email 2: fix the brief before video production.
  3. Email 3: avoid revision loops.
  4. Email 4: close the loop.
  5. Positive reply: ask what they are trying to explain and whether it is linked to a launch, campaign, investor, sales, or customer education need.
