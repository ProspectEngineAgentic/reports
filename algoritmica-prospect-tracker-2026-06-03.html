Just to clarify first: deeploans is an open-source project and we reach out to practitioners like you to get honest feedback from people who work with credit data every day.

We are building three things at Algoritmica:

1. deeploans - an open-source ETL framework for processing European loan-level data (ECB/ESMA-aligned). It automates the full pipeline - ingest, clean, standardize - so credit risk teams get clean, model-ready data without manual wrangling. Free, Apache 2.0, Python-based.

Directly relevant given your compliance and reporting background.

More here: algoritmica.ai/absbox-enterprise
