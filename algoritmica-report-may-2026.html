We sent 251 invitations and added 44 new connections, an acceptance rate of about 18%. That is a healthy rate for cold outreach to senior people in banking and asset management, where inboxes are crowded. The reply rate of 7.3% is normal for a cold sequence at this seniority. It takes several touches before a C-suite or MD-level buyer replies, and the sequence ran all five steps this period.

Total messages also reflect an active conversation backlog. Many connected prospects received M1 through M5 across the two weeks, so the 205 figure is cumulative across the full sequence, not 205 separate people.

The 3 declines are clean: polite, definitive, no friction in the thread. They do not indicate a messaging problem. All three conversations are closed and will not receive further messages.

The 6 neutral replies include a couple of off-topic inbound messages and one prospect who sent only their email address. No action is needed on those except logging.
