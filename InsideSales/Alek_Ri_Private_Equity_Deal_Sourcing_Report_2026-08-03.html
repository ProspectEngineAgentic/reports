Core Recommendation

Lead with deal sourcing, not lead generation.

For private equity, the real value is not the number of emails sent. The value is the number of credible founder conversations, referral partners, and companies that match the buyer's thesis.

The Five Step Deal Sourcing Model

Define the acquisition thesis
Clarify target sectors, company size, geography, ownership type, desired deal structure, and what international growth potential means in practice.
Build the company and founder map
Identify owners, co-founders, family business operators, exporters, SaaS founders, manufacturing leaders, and referral partners in the chosen markets.
Score each target for fit and timing
Rank companies by growth signals, international readiness, founder reachability, industry fit, ownership profile, and likely reason to talk now.
Start trusted conversations
Use LinkedIn, referral routes, and carefully controlled email where appropriate. The message should feel strategic, specific, and reputation safe.
Convert interest into qualified calls
Classify replies, separate direct opportunities from referral partners, manage follow ups, and hand Alek serious calls with context.
The campaign KPI should be qualified founder conversations and referral partners, not message volume.

Recommended Pilot Structure

30 Day Pilot

  • Choose one thesis such as South Asian founder led companies with international expansion potential.
  • Select two or three sectors where Alek has real appetite.
  • Build a vetted list of companies, founders, owners, and referral partners.
  • Prepare soft outreach that opens a strategic conversation without sounding like a forced acquisition pitch.
  • Run controlled outreach and classify all replies into direct opportunity, referral partner, later, not fit, or not interested.
Pilot Success Metrics
  • Qualified founder conversations
  • Referral partners with access to owners
  • Companies matching the acquisition thesis
  • Clear market response by sector
  • Documented objections and next best segment

Compliance And Reputation Safety

Alek specifically asked about strict email rules and laws. This should be treated as a trust test, not a small operational question.

Email control

Use accurate sender identity, relevant business targeting, clear subject lines, opt out handling, and immediate suppression after objection.

LinkedIn control

Keep activity measured, human reviewed, and relationship led. Avoid high volume behaviour that harms the buyer's reputation.

Message control

No hard ask to sell the company in the first message. Start with growth, strategic options, partnership, or expansion relevance.

For private equity, reputation protection is part of the product.

Important Positioning Guardrails

  • Do not say Prospect Engine already has a ready list of companies unless each company is validated.
  • Do not sell message volume. Sell vetted access to founders, owners, and referral partners.
  • Do not open conversations with "Do you want to sell your company?" That is too direct for most owners.
  • Do not promise acquisitions. Promise a controlled deal sourcing process and qualified conversations.
  • Use case studies carefully. Naciel and Hasan.VC are the closest. BDG and JapanIQ prove market entry logic, not private equity deal execution.
