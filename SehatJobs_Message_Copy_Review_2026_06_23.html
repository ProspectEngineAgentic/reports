Objection Replies

These are short answer drafts for common replies during LinkedIn or WhatsApp conversations.

Objection: We already work with agencies
That makes sense. Many hospitals already do. Where Sehat Jobs may still help is when you need a more active healthcare only pipeline, especially for nurses, and when candidate quality and follow through matter after the fee is paid.
Objection: Nurses leave after joining
That is exactly one of the gaps Santhya raised. Sehat Jobs prepares candidates before joining and keeps close contact after placement so the hospital is not left alone once the candidate starts.
Objection: Agencies are expensive
I understand. That is why Sehat Jobs has flexible options for smaller hires and mass hiring. I can share the right option once I understand whether your need is one off hiring, regular nursing pipeline, or a recruitment drive.
