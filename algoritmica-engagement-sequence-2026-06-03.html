How this changes from before

From cold-invite ladder to 1-1 curated engagement

On 2026-06-02 Luca clarified: "LinkedIn is not really the priority - we need 1-1 curated engagements. You can engage via LinkedIn with existing connections and invite to hackathon." This sequence replaces the old approach entirely.

Old approach
  • Volume: 26 silent invites per day from cold pools (Persona A Sheet4, Persona C)
  • Sent to 2nd and 3rd-degree contacts not yet connected
  • Generic opener assuming no existing relationship
  • Hackathon appeared mid-sequence as a warm-up step, not as the primary reason for outreach
  • M2-M6 follow-ups sent after invitation was accepted (days or weeks later)
  • Focus: contributor recruitment and OSS stars
New approach
  • Quality over quantity: hand-picked existing 1st-degree connections only
  • No new cold invites. Work with who already knows Farhan
  • Opener acknowledges the existing connection and explains why now
  • Hackathon registration is the single CTA from message 1 (register unlocks all the links and resources)
  • Shorter sequence: 2 to 3 touches maximum. Stop on any reply
  • Focus: drive hackathon registrations and repo contribution (the KPI Luca cares about)

Operational rules for this sequence

Before any send - mandatory checks

  • Existing connections only. Confirm FIRST_DEGREE via Unipile /users/{ACw}?account_id=SENDER_ID before any send. Do not message 2nd or 3rd-degree contacts in this sequence.
  • Live inbox check before every send. If the prospect's message is the most recent in the thread, stop. Any prior reply = stop that thread forever.
  • No duplicate sends. If Farhan has already sent a hackathon message to this person in the current series, skip.
  • Timing hard stops. Step 3 for any persona must be sent by June 8 at the absolute latest. After June 10 the hackathon CTA is expired. Do not send this sequence after June 10.
  • Luca call = Persona C, step 3 only. Never offer a Luca call to Persona A or B, and never before step 3 of Persona C.
  • On any reply: stop the sequence immediately. Draft a human response and send to CEO for review before replying. Do not auto-reply.
  • No emojis. No em dashes. No en dashes. Ranges written as "to". Farhan's voice is plain, direct, friendly.
  • Hackathon link: https://barcelona.aitinkerers.org/p/structured-finance-hackathon-2026 - verify it is still live and accepting registrations before the first send.
