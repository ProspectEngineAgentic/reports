Prospect Engine Inside Sales — Saiful Islam Saif. Date: 2026-05-27.

This is the operating system for Saiful's personal-LinkedIn pipeline engine. The whole point: every action on this profile is a live demo of what Prospect Engine sells. Sloppy outreach would contradict the pitch, so quality and rhythm are the product. Nothing is random; nothing is automated-and-forgotten.

1. Objective & success definition

Objective: Book qualified conversations with founders and revenue leaders in SG, AUS, DE, MY who own a number but lack the team/system to fill the funnel — and close them into PE retainers (PE caps at 10 clients, so a small number of right deals matters more than volume).

Definition of success (per quarter):
- A clean, scored, tiered list growing toward 500 verified prospects.
- A steady reply → conversation → discovery-call funnel with quality benchmarks (below).
- Saiful visible and credible: consistent content + genuine engagement, so outreach lands on a warmed audience.
- Zero compliance incidents, zero duplicate/automated-looking sends, zero off-ICP outreach.

4. Daily / weekly / monthly rhythm

Daily (the systematic rhythm — no missed steps)

  1. Listen — check Saiful's LinkedIn inbox + notifications. Any reply → handle as human conversation, stop sequences.
  2. Engage (nurture) — comment thoughtfully on 5–10 target prospects' posts; appreciate wins; react meaningfully. Follow new target company pages. This is real nurturing, not drive-by likes.
  3. Verify + score — push yesterday's DRAFT candidates through live verification → tier them.
  4. Connect — send 15–20 personalized connection requests to Tier 1/2 (warmed first).
  5. Follow up — send due personalized follow-ups to prior connects (per sequence timing, always after a fresh live-inbox check).
  6. Source — process one source slice to top up the DRAFT pool (per 03-RESEARCH_PLAN.md).
  7. Log — update the tracker; note every touch.

Weekly

  • Publish/maintain content cadence (see §6) and reply to all comments on Saiful's posts in his voice.
  • Re-score nurture pool + uncontacted Tier 1/2; dedupe; refresh list counts.
  • Review funnel metrics vs benchmarks; adjust angles/sequences for what's working.
  • Replenish verified queue if < 1 week of volume remains.
  • Short progress note to CEO.

Monthly

  • Pipeline + conversion report (CEO reviews before anything client-facing).
  • Market-by-market read: which angle/case study/segment is converting; reallocate effort.
  • Refresh 04-MARKET_RESEARCH.md facts if materially changed.

6. Content & presence (fuel for the funnel)

Outreach lands far better when the prospect has seen Saiful being useful. So content is part of the engine, not a side project:
- 2–3 posts/week in Saiful's voice (7th-grade English, no dashes, practitioner tone, proof by specifics). Pillars: outbound that feels human, GTM systems, AI-with-judgment, founder-as-bottleneck, real numbers from the 250+ campaigns.
- Engagement-first: comment on target prospects' and adjacent founders' posts daily. Appreciation, a sharp add, a question. This is nurturing and it surfaces warm leads.
- Reply to every comment on his posts, in voice.
- Keep content one batch ahead, fresh visual structure, shared for approval before publish (standing PE workflow).

Full content system can mirror the Opcom Content Engine pattern (research → draft → CEO approve → schedule).
