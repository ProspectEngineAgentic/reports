ICP & Target Personas — Prospect Engine Inside Sales

Markets in priority order: Singapore → Australia → Germany → Malaysia. Date: 2026-05-27.

This is the filter. A prospect must pass the firmographic fit below before we even check buying intent. If they fail fit, they never enter the list, no matter how strong the signal.

1. Ideal Customer Profile (firmographic fit)

Dimension In Out
Business type B2B companies that sell to other businesses B2C, retail, pure ecommerce, NGOs, govt
Primary segments B2B SaaS / software, marketing & creative agencies, recruitment platforms, logistics/supply-chain tech, data/AI, biotech/healthtech, professional services & consulting Enterprises with mature 10+ SDR teams (no need for us), solo freelancers (no budget)

Revenue clue Has paying customers and a revenue target, but thin or no dedicated GTM/SDR function —
Geography HQ or strong presence in SG, AUS, DE, MY Outside the 4 markets (park for later)

The one-line test: "Does this person own a revenue or growth number, and would a researched, trust-first pipeline (or an AI system that removes manual work) obviously help them in the next 90 days?" If yes → candidate. If no → drop.

2. Decision-maker personas (who we message)

Persona A — Founder / CEO (primary)

  • Titles: Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Managing Director
  • Owns the revenue number personally, often still doing sales themselves
  • Pain: "I am the bottleneck. Pipeline is inconsistent. I don't have time to build outbound."
  • Hook: founder-to-founder, "I built this 250+ times, here's the one thing I'd change about your top of funnel."
  • Service to lead with: Revenue + GTM strategy

Persona B — Revenue / Growth leader

  • Titles: Head/VP/Director of Sales, Revenue, Growth, Marketing, Demand Gen; CRO; CMO
  • Owns pipeline targets, has some team but it's stretched
  • Pain: "Reps spend time on admin/research, reply rates are dropping, I need more qualified meetings."
  • Hook: reply-rate / meeting-rate benchmark from a comparable company
  • Service to lead with: Revenue + AI automation (free the team from manual work)

Persona C — Agency owner / Partner

  • Titles: Founder/CEO/Partner of marketing, recruitment, consulting, dev agencies
  • Pain: "We sell pipeline/work for clients but our own is feast-or-famine; we're too busy to prospect for ourselves."
  • Hook: "You fill your clients' funnels. Who fills yours?" + the agency-specific case angle
  • Service to lead with: Revenue (done-for-you outbound) + Operations

Persona D — Ops / COO at a lean team (AI-automation entry)

  • Titles: COO, Head of Operations, Founder wearing the ops hat
  • Pain: manual reporting, spreadsheet chaos, no SOPs, founder is the system
  • Hook: "the parts of your business you don't have a team for"
  • Service to lead with: AI automation + Software + Operations

Persona A and C are the highest-converting for a founder-led pitch and should be the bulk of the list. B and D widen the funnel and open the AI-automation story.

3. Per-market notes (where to search, what resonates)

Singapore (priority 1 — strongest existing data + proven case)

  • Dense B2B SaaS, fintech, logistics-tech, IT services, and a huge agency scene.
  • Proof to lead with: Fr8Labs (SEA logistics) and Decube (data SaaS, SEA).
  • Existing raw data: B2B Prospecting/DATA/SG MY DATA/ (IT services, software dev, corporate training, buyer-intent sheets) + EMAIL DATA/Singapore.xlsx.
  • Tone: direct, ROI-first, time-zone aligned with Dhaka (easy same-day replies).

Australia (priority 2)

  • Strong SaaS, recruitment-tech, professional services, IT consulting.
  • Existing raw data: DATA/Others country/Aus, Dubai, Ireland it consult.xlsx + EMAIL DATA/Australia, dubai, ireland consulting email data_.xlsx.
  • Tone: plain, no-fluff, results-led. Australians dislike hype.

Germany (priority 3)

  • B2B SaaS / Mittelstand software, industrial-tech, agencies.
  • Existing raw data: PE Apollo Email List/PE Germany Apollo List.xlsx + DATA/Others country/Europe_CEO_Founder_1-10.xlsx.
  • Tone: precise, credible, low-hype, privacy-aware (GDPR). German buyers value specificity and proof over warmth. Consider EN first, DE if the contact's content is in German.

Malaysia (priority 4)

  • Growing SaaS, software houses, fintech, recruitment, professional services.
  • Existing raw data: DATA/SG MY DATA/ (combined SG+MY) + PE Apollo Email List/PE malaysia Apollo List.xlsx + DATA/Others country/Malaysia - Recruitements.xlsx.
  • Tone: warm + practical, relationship-led, value-for-money framing.

4. Exclusions (hard stop — never add)

  • Direct competitors (other lead-gen / GTM agencies) unless explicitly for partnership
  • Existing PE clients or active prospects already in another PE campaign (dedupe against repo client folders)
  • Anyone with an obvious "no budget" signal (student, intern, between roles, solopreneur with no team)
  • Companies in the 4 markets but clearly enterprise with mature in-house SDR orgs

This ICP feeds directly into the buying-intent index — fit is the gate, intent is the ranker.
