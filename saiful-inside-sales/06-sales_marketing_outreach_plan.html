Sales, Marketing & Outreach Plan

Prospect Engine Inside Sales — Saiful Islam Saif. Date: 2026-05-27.

How we turn a scored, tiered list into booked conversations and retainers — on LinkedIn, through Saiful's own profile, plus content and genuine engagement. Pairs with 05 (scoring), 04 (market specifics), and 07-OUTREACH_COPY.md (the actual words).

Three hard rules govern everything here: (1) LinkedIn only — every touch runs through Saiful's LinkedIn profile (CEO mandate, 2026-05-27); (2) no automated/templated message ever — each touch is hand-fit to the person's business, role, and pain; (3) any reply stops the sequence and becomes a human conversation.

2. The engagement-first motion (nurture before the ask)

Before a Tier-1 prospect ever gets a connection request, we warm them for 3–5 days:
- Follow them and their company page.
- Comment on 1–2 of their recent posts — a sharp, specific add or question, never "great post."
- React meaningfully; appreciate a genuine win (a raise, a launch, an award).
- Let them see Saiful's name as a useful peer before the request.

This continues after connecting too. Nurturing is ongoing: we stay present on their content, celebrate milestones, and drop value with no ask. This is what converts Tier 3 → Tier 2 and keeps Tier 1 warm between touches. It is also the single biggest lift to acceptance and reply rates.

3. Outreach sequences

All timings assume no reply at each step; a reply ends the sequence. Every step is personalized — the structure is fixed, the words are not.

The LinkedIn sequence (the only sequence, all markets)

Everything happens inside LinkedIn: the connection request, the DMs, and the ongoing engagement (comments, reactions, following pages). No email, no WhatsApp.

4. Message principles (Saiful's voice)

From 00-PERSONA_AND_COMPANY.md:
- 7th-grade English. No dashes, no em dashes. Human, plain, confident.
- Insight-led. Open with a specific observation about their business. Never "hope this finds you well," "just checking in," "quick question."
- Practitioner, not vendor. A founder who has run this 250+ times.
- One idea per message. One soft ask. Never pitch in the first touch.
- Proof by specifics (numbers, named outcomes), never adjectives.
- Match the case study to the prospect: Decube (data/AI SaaS, SEA), Fr8Labs (SEA logistics), AutoRetouch (SaaS product growth).

6. Conversation → call → close

  • On any reply: stop the sequence. Respond as a human, in voice, within the same working day (timezone-friendly for SG/MY especially). Goal of the conversation, not the message, is a short discovery call.
  • Discovery call: Saiful takes every first call (boutique, founder-led). Honest audit framing, not a pitch.
  • Proposal: same-day proposal is a PE differentiator — use it.
  • Scarcity is real: PE caps at 10 clients ("2 of 10 slots open" style) — use truthfully, never as a fake tactic.

7. Tracking & reporting

  • Source of truth: the master list / tracker (schema in 03-RESEARCH_PLAN.md §4), one row per prospect, every touch logged with date + channel + outcome.
  • Stop-on-reply enforced by checking the live inbox before every send (standing PE invariant — never trust the tracker alone).
  • Weekly: funnel metrics vs benchmarks (02-PROJECT_PLAN.md §5).
  • Monthly: pipeline report, CEO reviews before any client-facing summary.
