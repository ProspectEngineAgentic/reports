Positioning & Top Voice Plan

Prospect Engine Inside Sales — Saiful Islam Saif. Date: 2026-05-27.

Goal (CEO directive 2026-05-27): establish Saiful as an industry pioneer, a problem-solver, and a LinkedIn Top Voice. Outreach books meetings; this is what makes the outreach land warm and pulls inbound. The two run together: a warmed, credible audience replies to outreach; a visible problem-solver gets prospects coming to him.

1. The position to own

Everyone in Saiful's feed is selling an AI-native GTM tool or course (Yalc, Kakiyo, Conigma, Bitscale, Clay courses — see Knowledge/KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md). They are vendors and teachers. Saiful's edge is different and stronger:

The operator who actually runs it. 250+ campaigns, 100+ clients, 24 markets, a real agency built on Claude. Not "here is a tool," but "here is what happens when you run this for real, at scale, for paying clients."

Positioning line: practitioner > vendor. Proof by specifics, judgment over hype, honest about what breaks. That is what earns "pioneer" and "problem-solver," and Top Voice follows credibility, not the other way around.

2. Content pillars (each is a recurring theme, all operator-voiced)

  1. AI-native GTM, for real. Running GTM on Claude: what works, what breaks, where judgment beats automation. (Rides the hottest topic in his network with lived experience.)
  2. The founder bottleneck. Founder-as-SDR is the most expensive sales strategy; how to build the system instead.
  3. Trust-first outbound. Why fewer, researched messages beat volume; the death of template blasting.
  4. Signals & intent. Reading buying intent like a human (LinkedIn, Reddit, triggers) instead of buying lists.
  5. Behind the agency. Real numbers and lessons from 250+ campaigns (Decube 129 SQLs, Fr8Labs 60 meetings, etc.) — proof, not theory.

Voice rules (from 00): 7th-grade English, no dashes, human, one idea, proof by specifics, a question at the end. War-story / first-person format performs best ("I watched...", "I spent...", "After 250 campaigns...").

3. The engagement engine (this is where Top Voice is won)

Top Voice and pioneer status come from being present and sharp in the conversations already happening — not just posting.
- Daily: thoughtful comments on 5–10 posts in his lane (GTM, AI/Claude, founder-led sales). A real add or a sharp question, never "great post." This is what currently fills his inbox — we make it deliberate and on-message.
- Engage the builders: the people DMing him (Yalc/Othmane, the Claude-GTM crowd) are his peer network. Real peer conversations (not buying their tools) raise his profile and feed content.
- Reply to every comment on his own posts, in voice (concede then restate, no hard CTA).
- Publish 3–4x/week across the pillars, keep one batch ahead, fresh visuals, CEO-approved before publishing.

4. How the inbox feeds content (closing the loop)

Every useful thing shared with Saiful (the Knowledge Base) becomes raw material:
- A tool/course someone pitches → a post on "I tried X, here is the operator's verdict."
- A question a prospect/peer asks → a post answering it publicly (problem-solver in action).
- A trend in the DMs (this week: Claude-for-GTM) → a sharp, experience-backed POV that rides the wave.

So the inbox is not just triage; it is a content and intelligence stream. Knowledge in → POV out → visibility up → warmer outreach + inbound.

5. Metrics for the Top Voice goal

  • Posts/week (target 3–4), comments/week on others (target 25–40), reply rate on own posts (100%).
  • Profile views, post impressions, follower growth, search appearances (track monthly).
  • Inbound conversations from content (the real prize): count and tag in the inbox tracker.
  • LinkedIn "Top Voice" badge in a relevant topic = a lagging indicator of the above done consistently.
