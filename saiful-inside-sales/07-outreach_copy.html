Outreach Copy — Saiful's Voice

Prospect Engine Inside Sales. Date: 2026-05-27.

These are starting structures, not blast templates. The shape is fixed; the words get hand-fit to each prospect. Every [bracket] must be filled with something specific you actually saw on their profile/company/news. If you can't fill a bracket with something real, do more research before sending. Voice rules: 7th-grade English, no dashes, no em dashes, human, confident, practitioner not vendor, one idea, one soft ask, proof by specifics. Any reply ends the sequence and becomes a real conversation.

Proof points to slot in (use the closest match):
- Decube (data/AI SaaS, SEA): 129 SQLs, 68 meetings, 35% connect rate in 8 months.
- Fr8Labs (SEA logistics): 178 leads, 60 meetings across Southeast Asia.
- AutoRetouch (SaaS product growth): 17.6% user growth, 33,825+ users, 20.9% session lift.

A. Persona A — Founder / CEO

Connection note (no pitch, < 300 chars)

Hi [First], I have been following what [Company] is building in [specific space]. I run outbound and GTM for founders and I liked [specific thing you saw]. Would be good to connect.

M1 (1–2 days after accept)

Thanks for connecting [First]. I work with founders who own the number but do not have a full sales team yet. Most of them are still doing their own outreach. I noticed [specific signal, e.g. you just raised / you are hiring your first AE]. When I ran this for [Decube/Fr8Labs], we got [specific result]. Curious how you are handling pipeline right now.

M2 (+4 days, value)

One thing that helped [comparable company]: we stopped sending volume and started sending fewer, researched messages. That moved their connect-to-meeting rate to [X]. Happy to share the exact play if it is useful for [Company].

M3 (+7 days, soft ask)

[First], would a short 15 minute call be worth it? I can walk you through what I would change about [Company]'s top of funnel. No pitch, just an honest read.

M4 (break-up, +10 days)

I will stop here so I am not crowding your inbox. If pipeline ever gets noisy, ping me and I will share the system we use. Rooting for [Company].

B. Persona B — Revenue / Growth leader

Connection note

Hi [First], I saw you lead [revenue/growth] at [Company]. I work on reply-rate and meeting-rate systems for B2B teams and I had a thought about [specific thing]. Would like to connect.

M1

Thanks [First]. Reply rates are dropping across the board right now, mostly because reps spend more time on admin than on the actual message. We rebuilt this for [Decube/AutoRetouch] and got [result]. How is your team splitting research vs sending today?

M2 (value)

The fix that worked: let an AI system do the research and drafting, let the rep do the judgment. That freed [comparable team] to run more conversations without more headcount. Want the benchmark numbers from a team like yours?

M3 (soft ask)

Worth a quick 15 minutes to compare notes on what is working in [their market] right now? Happy to share what we see across [24] markets.

M4 (break-up)

I will leave it there. If you ever want a second set of eyes on the sequence, I am one message away.

C. Persona C — Agency owner / Partner

Connection note

Hi [First], [Agency] does great work filling your clients' funnels. I help agencies fill their own, which is the part that always slips. Would be good to connect.

M1

Thanks [First]. Most agency owners I talk to say the same thing. Client work always wins, so their own pipeline goes feast or famine. We built a done-for-you outbound engine for [Fr8Labs/AutoRetouch] that ran in the background while they stayed on delivery. How are you handling your own pipeline right now?

M2 (value)

The trick was making it feel human at scale, not automated. Researched messages, real follow-up, no spam from your brand. That protected [agency]'s reputation while still booking meetings. Happy to show the setup.

M3 (soft ask)

Would a short call be useful? I can show you the exact engine and what it would look like running quietly behind [Agency].

M4 (break-up)

I will stop here. When your own pipeline becomes the priority, ping me and I will share the playbook.

D. Persona D — Ops / COO (AI-automation entry)

Connection note

Hi [First], I saw you run operations at [Company]. I build AI systems that take over the manual parts a lean team should not be doing by hand. Had a thought about [specific manual thing]. Would like to connect.

M1

Thanks [First]. A lot of lean teams run their reporting and follow-up in spreadsheets, and the founder ends up being the system. We build Claude based systems that absorb that work. For [comparable company] it removed hours of manual ops every week. What is the most repetitive thing your team still does by hand?

M2 (value)

We usually start with the one workflow that eats the most time and automate just that, so there is a quick win before anything big. Want me to point at the one I would automate first for [Company]?

M3 (soft ask)

Worth 15 minutes to map where a simple AI system would save your team the most time? No pitch, just a map.

M4 (break-up)

I will leave it there. When the manual work piles up, I am happy to help you offload it.

E. Market flavor notes (adjust tone, keep structure)

  • Singapore: direct, numbers-first. Lead with the result. Same-day replies are realistic.
  • Australia: plain, no hype, no adjectives. Be straight about why you reached out. Numbers win.
  • Germany (LinkedIn only): precision and proof. Open with a real researched observation, never "I came across your profile." One claim, one question, zero enthusiasm. English first; German only if all their content is German. Never email cold.
  • Malaysia: warm and relationship-led. Soft asks. Give something useful before asking. WhatsApp only after connecting.

G. Engagement / nurture snippets (comments, appreciation)

Use to warm prospects before and between touches. Specific, never generic.
- On a funding post: "Congrats [First]. Curious which market you go after first with the new raise."
- On a product launch: "This is sharp. The [specific feature] is the part most teams skip. How are early users reacting?"
- On a hiring post (Head of Sales/SDR): "Good hire to make now. The first sales hire either builds the system or inherits the chaos. Rooting for [Company]."
- On a thoughtful post: "Agree with [their point]. The part people miss is [specific add]."

Never "great post" or a bare emoji. Every comment earns the next touch.
