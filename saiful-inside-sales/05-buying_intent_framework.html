Buying-Intent Framework & Scoring Index

Prospect Engine Inside Sales — Saiful Islam Saif. Date: 2026-05-27.

The rule of this project: Fit is the gate. Intent is the ranker. No prospect enters the list on a whim. A name is only added when (a) it passes the firmographic ICP gate in 01-ICP_AND_PERSONAS.md, and (b) we can point to at least one real, observed reason this person likely needs Prospect Engine in the next 90 days. "Looks like a founder" is not a reason. A specific signal is.

1. How B2B buying intent actually works (the research)

Buying intent for a service like ours is rarely a single "I want to buy" moment. It is a stack of weaker signals that, combined, say "this person has a problem we solve, a budget to solve it, and a reason to act now." The industry groups intent into three layers:

Layer What it is Where we read it Strength
First-party Signals the prospect creates about themselves Their LinkedIn posts, company site, job posts, profile changes, their content/comments Strongest (specific to them)
Second-party Signals from their network/company context Funding news, hiring pages, leadership changes, reviews, partner announcements Medium
Third-party Aggregated behavioral/topic data Intent-data vendors (Bombora, G2, etc.), tech-stack tools Weak alone, useful for ranking

We are a boutique, research-first shop, not a volume buyer of third-party intent data. So our edge is first-party signal reading done by a human — exactly what Saiful sells. Our intent engine leans on signals we can verify with our own eyes on a profile or a company page, and treats third-party data as a tie-breaker, not a trigger.

The 8 signal categories we score (the "buying parameters")

  1. Pipeline pain (stated) — they posted/wrote about inconsistent pipeline, slow growth, "we need leads," reply rates dropping, founder still selling.
  2. Growth pressure (funded/target) — recent raise (seed–Series B), new revenue target, expansion announcement, new market entry.
  3. Hiring signal — actively hiring SDR/BDR/AE/Head of Sales/Growth/Marketing (means they believe in outbound but may lack the system; or are about to build it badly).
  4. Founder-led-sales signal — small team, no visible SDR/sales org, founder's recent activity shows them doing their own outreach/demos.
  5. Manual-ops / lean-team signal — "we do it all in spreadsheets," no reporting, repetitive ops a Claude system could absorb (the AI-automation entry).
  6. Channel-presence gap — invisible founder/brand, no inbound, weak or no content, dormant company page (the Presence pitch).
  7. Trigger event — new role (just became CEO/Head of Sales), new product launch, repositioning, recent agency churn ("we tried an agency, it didn't work"), event/award.
  8. Engagement reachability — active on LinkedIn (posts/comments in last 30 days), so a human, personalized touch will actually be seen and can be warmed first.

2. The Buying-Intent Index (scoring model)

Each prospect gets a score out of 100. Two parts: a Fit multiplier (the gate) and an Intent score (the ranker).

Step 1 — Fit gate (pass/fail, then a 0.5–1.0 quality multiplier)

A prospect must pass the ICP gate to be scored at all. Within "pass," we grade fit quality:

Fit grade Meaning Multiplier
A — bullseye B2B SaaS/agency, 11–50 emp, post-seed–Series B, in a priority market, decision-maker persona ×1.0
B — strong Adjacent segment or 51–200 emp or persona B/D, still clearly in ICP ×0.85
C — edge Meets the letter of the ICP but a weaker fit (e.g., 5–10 emp, secondary market) ×0.7
Fail Misses a hard gate or hits an exclusion ×0 → not added

Step 2 — Intent score (0–100, before multiplier)

Score the 8 parameters. Weighted so that stated pain and trigger events (the strongest, most actionable) count most:

# Parameter Max pts How to score
1 Pipeline pain (stated) 20 20 if explicit in their words; 10 if strongly implied
2 Growth pressure / funded 18 18 if raise/target in last 6 mo; 9 if 6–12 mo
3 Trigger event 16 16 for a fresh, specific trigger; 8 if older/soft
4 Founder-led-sales 12 12 if clearly doing own sales, no SDR org
5 Hiring signal 12 12 if hiring sales/growth now; 6 if recently
6 Manual-ops / lean-team 8 8 if clear AI-automation opening
7 Channel-presence gap 8 8 if invisible/dormant; 4 if thin
8 Engagement reachability 6 6 if active last 30 days; 3 if last 90
Total 100

Step 3 — Final score & tiering

Final = Intent score × Fit multiplier.

Tier Final score What it means Action
Tier 1 — Hot 70–100 Strong fit + multiple live signals Front of queue. Warm first (engage on their content 3–5 days), then personalized connect. Saiful-voice, hand-written.
Tier 2 — Warm 45–69 Good fit + 1–2 signals Standard queue. Personalized connect after a quick warm-up touch.
Tier 3 — Watch 25–44 Fits but thin signal Nurture-only: follow, engage, wait for a trigger. Do not pitch yet.
Drop <25 Weak Park. Re-score if a trigger appears.

No prospect goes into an outreach sequence below Tier 2 unless a new trigger lifts them. Tier 3 lives in the nurture pool (we follow + engage and wait).

3. Where each signal is read (the verification checklist)

For every prospect, before they move from DRAFT to a verified, scored row, a human (or Saiful) confirms on the live profile/page:

  • [ ] Title + company still match the list (titles drift — verify live, per standing rule).
  • [ ] Headcount still in range (LinkedIn "People" tab / company page).
  • [ ] Last 3 posts / comments read — capture the live signal + a personal hook.
  • [ ] Company page: recent posts, hiring tab, "About" for stage/size.
  • [ ] Funding / news: Crunchbase / recent press / their own "we raised" post.
  • [ ] Persona confirmed (A/B/C/D) and the right service pillar chosen.
  • [ ] Dedupe: not an existing PE client or active prospect in another PE campaign.
  • [ ] Exclusions: not a competitor (other lead-gen/GTM agency), not B2C, not enterprise-with-mature-SDR.

Output of this step: the prospect's row gets intent_score, fit_grade, final_score, tier, live_signal (the actual observed hook), and personal_hook (the one specific thing we'll open with). Only then is it sendable.

4. Sourcing → scoring pipeline (so nothing is random)

Source pool (accelerators, VC portfolios, directories, classic/SN search, existing PE data)
        │
        ▼
[ FIT GATE ]  ── fail ──► drop / park
        │ pass
        ▼
[ QUICK INTENT PASS ]  (desk research: funding, hiring, posts)
        │
        ▼
[ LIVE VERIFICATION ]  (checklist above, on the real profile)
        │
        ▼
[ SCORE & TIER ]  → verified queue, ranked by final_score
        │
        ▼
[ NURTURE or OUTREACH ]  per tier

This is the discipline behind the 500. We do not "find 500 names." We run pools through this pipeline until 500 scored, verified, tiered prospects exist. The starter list (Leads/lead_list_v1.csv) is the first batch through the top of this pipe — every row is real and fit-passed, and is tagged DRAFT pending the live-verification + scoring step (blocked today only by the Sales Nav reconnect for richer search; classic search + manual verification can proceed now).

5. Re-scoring cadence

Intent decays and triggers appear. Re-score:
- Tier 3 / nurture pool: monthly, or instantly when a trigger fires (new raise, new role, a pipeline-pain post we see while engaging).
- Tier 1/2 not yet contacted: weekly (freshness matters most when intent is high).
- Contacted, no reply: intent re-read before each follow-up; any reply = stop the sequence and switch to human conversation.
