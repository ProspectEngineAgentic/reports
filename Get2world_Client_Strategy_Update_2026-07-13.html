Get2world Lead Generation Plan

Updated execution plan for ZFDM and CallDoc outreach | July 2026

Executive Summary

We are moving the campaign from a broad outreach approach into two focused tracks. The goal is to create more relevant conversations with the right decision makers, then move only the qualified conversations toward a short meeting.

The new structure is built around two clear buyer situations: healthcare leaders reacting to the current sick-note discussion, and German SME owners or HR leaders who want simpler time tracking and less manual administration.

Database Structure

One master database

We will keep one clean master database as the source of truth. This prevents duplicate outreach, keeps the contact history clean, and lets us track which segment each person belongs to.

Two campaign views

From the master database, we use two separate campaign views. This lets each segment get the right opening question, follow-up logic, and reporting without mixing the message angle.

How Replies Will Be Handled

  1. First we understand the prospect's current process.
  2. We ask one simple diagnosis question instead of pitching too early.
  3. If they mention phone pressure or appointment pressure, we continue with the CallDoc angle.
  4. If they mention absences, overtime, staff planning, proof, or monthly software cost, we continue with the ZFDM angle.
  5. Only when there is a real operational gap do we suggest a short call.
