Campaign Insights

EU AI Act Angle — Strong Hook for Persona B

The compliance messaging (high-risk AI classification, audit trail for loan data) is generating the most engagement among credit risk and quant profiles. Persona B has the highest reply rate of any segment.

Hybrid Format — Unlocks More Prospects

Several prospects had gone quiet assuming the event was in-person only. Clarifying the virtual phase (May 27–June 9) has re-opened conversations and should be emphasised in all future messages.

Judge Invitation Works for Persona C

Senior professionals respond better to a judge invitation than a general attendee ask. The framing is credibility-affirming and generates more meaningful replies at the Head of Data / AI level.

Accuracy Data Builds Instant Credibility

The 16–24% accuracy improvement stat vs IRB models from 50+ European banks is consistently referenced in positive replies. Specific, testable, and memorable — continue leading with this in sequences.

Persona C — Follow-up Pipeline Growing

Persona C has the highest invite volume (524) but 0.6% reply rate. With 80 now on M2 and 20 on M3, the follow-up pipeline is maturing. The judge angle in M2 is the key lever to monitor.

Email Campaigns Paused

Both Persona A and Persona C email campaigns are currently paused. 199 of 490 Persona A emails have been delivered. Persona C has not yet been sent. Recommend reviewing and reactivating when ready.
