LinkedIn Message Flow

M1 after accept

Most product videos fail before production starts.

The team does not have an animation problem yet.
They have a story problem.

I saw {{company}} is working in {{specific_product_area}}.

If your team had to explain that product to a buyer in 60 seconds, where would they get stuck first?

M2

Quick thought, {{first_name}}.

A polished video can still fail if the product story is unclear.

That usually shows up as script rewrites, storyboard changes, and sales teams still explaining everything from scratch.

Triken runs a 90 minute Product Story Mapping Session before production so the team has the blueprint first.

Is {{company}} planning a launch, campaign, or product video this quarter?

M3

This is the simple version.

Before spending 10k to 18k on animation, the team spends 90 minutes mapping the story.

The output is a Product Blueprint.

Buyer logic.
Scene flow.
Message hierarchy.
Confusion points.
Visual direction.

If the product is complex, this saves the expensive part from starting on a weak brief.

Worth looking at for {{company}}?

Qualification before meeting

That sounds relevant.

Before I suggest time with Vignesh, can I check two things?

What product or campaign are you trying to explain better?

And are you looking for the story map first, the full animation production, or both?

Email Flow

Email 1
Subject: before the video brief

Hi {{first_name}},

Most product videos fail before animation starts.

The brief is unclear.
The buyer is not mapped.
The team does not agree on what must be understood first.

Then the studio starts producing, and every unclear thought becomes a revision.

Triken helps complex product teams fix this with a 90 minute Product Story Mapping Session.

It turns decks, PDFs, product notes, and website copy into a same day Product Blueprint before production starts.

Is {{company}} planning a product video, launch, or campaign where that would matter?

Best,
Vignesh

Email 2
Subject: the expensive part

Hi {{first_name}},

The expensive part is not always the animation.

It is starting animation before the story is clear.

That is when scripts get rewritten.
Storyboards get pulled apart.
Product, sales, and marketing all give different feedback.

The Product Story Mapping Session is built to stop that before it happens.

Would a clear product blueprint be useful before your next campaign?

Best,
Vignesh

Email 3
Subject: one question

Hi {{first_name}},

One useful question before making a product video:

What does the buyer need to understand before they care?

If that answer is not clear, the video becomes a visual version of internal language.

Triken maps that answer first, then production has a clean path.

Is that something your team is working through now?

Best,
Vignesh

Email 4
Subject: close the loop

Hi {{first_name}},

I will close the loop after this.

If there is no product video, launch, or campaign coming up, the timing may be wrong.

If there is one coming, the safest first step is to map the story before production starts.

Should I leave this for now, or is it worth a short look?

Best,
Vignesh
