What this is, and why

Robert has a list of 91 people he once had a real conversation with, captured in his Project Master File. These are not fresh cold leads. They replied to him in the past with interest, a referral, a partnership idea, or a meeting that then lapsed, and the thread has gone quiet since roughly last November.

The agenda is simple: warm these relationships back up with a short, human hello in Robert's own voice. We acknowledge the time gap, share one soft update (his new ITIL Master across all six modules at trainer level, and that he is busy delivering courses), and ask one light question about how they are doing now. This is relationship maintenance, not a sales pitch. Robert handles any warm reply himself.

Most of this list are not end clients. Many are training companies, peers, or partners who once wanted Robert as a trainer or partner. So each message is tailored to the relationship, not blasted with the same line.

How each send will run (the safe steps)

  • Oldest conversation first. This batch covers the 12 longest-dormant, from 531 down to 407 days.
  • Live inbox check before every single send. If they have replied, or Robert has replied since, we skip and stop that person forever.
  • Angle tailored per person: partner, peer, or buyer (see the colour tags below).
  • Job changes verified. Where the person moved roles, the old company hook is dropped and the message is rewritten for their new world.
  • Same day warm-up: from Robert's profile we like and leave one short human comment on their recent post.
  • Pace: 12 per day, 5 minutes apart, only inside the prospect's local working hours. One pass through all 71 takes about 5 to 7 working days.
  • Voice: British English, 7th grade, no dashes, warm, one light question, signed Robert. Every message worded differently.
