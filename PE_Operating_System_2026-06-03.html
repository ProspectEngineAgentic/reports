1 · The goal

Turn ProspectEngine into a productized agency that two people can run. One person + Claude runs the entire client-delivery operation; one person + Claude runs the entire InsideSales (our own outbound). We are an agency — we do the work; it is AI-powered (we research every lead with AI, personalize every message with Claude). Not "AI does it for you." We are accountable for results.

2 · The shape — one system, two seats

The whole business is a single operating system stamped into two seats:

Seat A

Client Delivery

The retainer book (10 clients). Each client = its own workspace. We deliver results: booked meetings for the client.

Seat B

InsideSales

PE's own outbound — 20–30 SDR LinkedIn IDs. We win PE its own retainers.

HUMAN (steers · approves · decides) — a few minutes a day │ one daily batch (chat) + weekly review CLAUDE = a COO per seat ├── runs the daily loop, coordinates the team ├── directs the agent-employees └── rides the safety rails (enforced automatically) │ runs on always-on LOOPS — with or without a human

6 · How it actually runs — one command + loops

The operator types one command:

coo PDCA → review everything → ask (one batch) → start + run on loops

From there it self-drives on a set of always-on loops (different jobs, different intervals):

LoopEvery"Asks itself…"
Replies~10 minany new replies? → triage, draft/book, stop the sequence on a reply
Task-watch~30 minthis task we started — finished? → advance or retry
Cadence~30 minanyone due for M1/M2/M3? → live-check, send
Inviteshourlyqueue full + cap left? → send; if low, ask for a Sales Nav link
Contentdailynext batch ready + approved? → publish at local 10:00
Engagement~30 minnew comments? → reply + boost
Daily batch1×/dayassemble "your 5 minutes"
The core rule: a missing human answer parks one item — it never stalls the engine. No Sales Nav link yet? Only invites pause; replies, cadence, content and engagement keep running. With zero human input the system still answers replies, advances tasks, runs cadence, engages, reports, and learns — indefinitely.

7 · Self-healing (behaves like a human team)

The system detects, contains, and recovers from its own failures:

LoopDetectsDoes automatically
Watchdogstuck tasks, silent loops, failed sendsretry with backoff → pause the lane + alert
Account-healthLinkedIn checkpoint/disconnect, accept-rate crashpause that account's lane to protect it, flag re-auth, log it

The recovery chain: detect → contain → log the mistake → L&D teaches the team → the board merges the lesson. Every failure makes the system more reliable, not less.

8 · Learning & improvement (it gets better every cycle)

RUN → MEASURE → ANALYZE → SIMULATE → DECIDE → UPDATE → (RUN better)
  • The flywheel: we measure every outcome → the analyst finds what works → the client-persona twin pressure-tests it from the client's side → the board decides → the briefs update → the next cycle runs sharper.
  • ICP keeps tightening: we correlate who actually replied and booked against the ICP and sharpen the targeting each cycle. "These leads are too junior" becomes a concrete ICP edit, not a recurring complaint.
  • Learning & Development (L&D): like a human team that never repeats a mistake — every mistake is logged blamelessly, root-caused, turned into a lesson, and taught to the whole team (shared playbooks + agent instructions). On a loop, with or without anyone watching.

9 · The standing board (always deliberating)

A multi-lens decision ritual that convenes every cycle and on triggers (a metric drop, a complaint, new client info, a renewal). Nine lenses — CEO, COO, Growth, CMO, Design, CTO, AI Architect, CFO, and the Customer lens (voiced by the client's digital twin, so every decision is tested against "what would the client say?"). It logs a brief and flags what needs the CEO.

10 · Seat A — the clients

10 active retainers, each on the identical standardized structure (a master file + numbered folders + per-role briefs). Adding a client = clone the template, fill the briefs, connect the account — then it runs on the same loop as everyone else.

Active: PDCA · Opcom · Algoritmica · Get2world · · Inccrease · CodeCapsule · VectorShield · TruGen · CoNEXTion. Four are at a renewal-edge and get driven in the weekly review.

11 · Seat B — InsideSales (the 20–30 IDs)

One operator runs many SDR LinkedIn IDs without it becoming many jobs. The trick: don't manage 30 strategies — manage 5 country playbooks × per-ID personalization. Each ID is a mini-persona that inherits its country pod's ICP and content pillars; the agents personalize underneath; the operator approves at the pod level (~5 decisions), not per ID.

  • Content for each ID: the pod sets 4–5 content pillars; the copywriter + designer produce each ID's posts from {pillars × that ID's angle}, with structure varied so the IDs never look like clones (authenticity and safety).
  • Sales Nav links by pod, not by ID: the operator drops ~5 links; the researcher splits + dedups across each pod's IDs.
  • Safety is the #1 risk at 30 accounts: warmup ramp per ID, accept-rate auto-throttle, account-health auto-pause, never identical copy.
